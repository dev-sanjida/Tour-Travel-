About this cruise

This seven-night cruise leaves from Baltimore and sails south to the Bahamas, with relaxing days at sea on the way down and on the way back. You spend a full day in Nassau, where you can wander the colorful old town, tour the historic forts or simply head to the beach. The ship then calls at a private island for snorkeling, kayaking and lunch on the sand, before a final stop at Freeport on Grand Bahama.

Meals are included at the main restaurant, the buffet and the poolside grill, while the steakhouse and the sushi bar can be booked for a small extra charge. Evenings bring live shows in the theater, music in the lounges and a comedy club for adults, and kids and teens have their own supervised clubs from morning until late.
