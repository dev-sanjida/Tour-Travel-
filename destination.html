Discover Georgia

Mountain villages, 8,000 years of winemaking and the warmest welcome in the Caucasus.

Georgia packs a lot into a small country. Tbilisi feels like a city with a secret: sulfur baths, tiny wine bars and an old town that tumbles down to the river. Three hours north, the road climbs into the Caucasus and the church at Gergeti sits above the clouds.

East of the capital, Kakheti makes wine the way it has for thousands of years, in clay jars buried underground. And in summer, Batumi turns into a Black Sea beach town.

Good to know

  • PlugsType C and F, 220 V. Bring a European adapter.
  • PayingCards work in cities; carry lari for villages and markets.
  • TippingRestaurants often add 10%; leave a little extra for great service.
  • ChurchesCover shoulders and knees; scarves are lent at the door.

A first trip in 10 nights

Our most booked route covers the capital, the mountains, wine country and the coast.

  1. 3 nights

    Tbilisi

    The capital: sulfur baths, wooden balconies and late dinners.

  2. 2 nights

    Kazbegi

    The hilltop church under Mount Kazbek, three hours north.

  3. 2 nights

    Kakheti

    Wine country: clay-jar cellars and the walled town of Sighnaghi.

  4. 3 nights

    Batumi

    Black Sea beaches and botanical gardens in summer.

Getting there

  • Two main airports

    Tbilisi (TBS) for the capital and the east; Kutaisi (KUT) for budget flights and the west.

  • From Europe and the US

    Direct flights from many European cities in about 4 hours. From New York, one stop and about 13 hours.

  • Getting around

    A fast train links Tbilisi and Batumi in 5 hours. For the mountains, hire a car or a driver for the day.

Questions travelers ask

Short answers from our Caucasus specialists.

Is Georgia safe to visit?

Yes. Georgia is one of the safest countries in the region, and Tbilisi is easy to walk around at night. Take the usual care with bags in busy places, and drive carefully on mountain roads.

Do I need a visa?

Citizens of the US, the UK, Canada and the EU can stay up to one year without a visa. Your passport should be valid for the whole stay.

How many days do I need?

Five days covers Tbilisi and a trip to Kazbegi. With ten nights you can add wine country and the Black Sea coast without rushing.

Is it easy to travel without speaking Georgian?

In Tbilisi and Batumi most young people speak English. In villages, a few words of Russian help, and a translation app does the rest. Our drivers and guides all speak English.

Can I visit the mountains in winter?

Gudauri is a ski resort from December to April. The road to Kazbegi stays open most days, but snow can close it for a few hours; plan a spare day.
