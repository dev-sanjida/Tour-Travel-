Popular questions

12 answers shown

How do I change or cancel my booking?

Open the confirmation email and choose Manage booking, or call us. You will see any fees and your refund before you confirm. Fully refundable stays can be canceled free of charge until the date shown on your booking.

Was this helpful?
Where is my confirmation?

We email it as soon as you book, from support@example.com. Check your spam folder, or ask us to send it again by phone or through the contact form. Have your confirmation number ready: it starts with TA-.

Was this helpful?
When will I get my refund?

Refunds go back to the card you paid with within 5 to 7 working days. Some banks take up to 10 days to show it on your statement.

Was this helpful?
How much baggage can I bring?

It depends on the fare. Your included bags are listed on the review page and in your confirmation. Adding a bag before you fly is cheaper than paying at the airport.

Was this helpful?
Can I check in early?

Early check-in depends on the hotel and how busy it is. Tell us your arrival time and we will ask for you; some hotels charge for it.

Was this helpful?
Which currencies can I pay in?

You can pay in US dollars, euros or pounds by card. Prices on the site are shown in US dollars and include taxes, unless a local city tax is paid at the hotel.

Was this helpful?
What do I need to pick up a rental car?

Your driving license, a credit card in the main driver’s name and your booking confirmation. Some countries also ask for an international driving permit.

Was this helpful?
Which documents do I need for a cruise?

A passport valid for six months after you return, and any visas for the ports you visit. We send a checklist with your cruise tickets three weeks before you sail.

Was this helpful?
Can I change the name on a ticket?

Most airlines allow a small spelling fix for free. A full name change usually means a new ticket. Call us and we will check what your fare allows.

Was this helpful?
Can I book for someone else?

Yes. Enter the traveler’s details exactly as on their passport, and your own email for the confirmation. You can pay with your own card.

Was this helpful?
How do I stop the newsletter?

Use the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any email, or write to us. Booking emails will still arrive. Read how we use your data in our privacy policy.

Was this helpful?
Are city taxes included in the price?

Taxes we collect are included. Some cities add a small tourist tax that you pay at the hotel; it is listed on the booking page before you pay.
