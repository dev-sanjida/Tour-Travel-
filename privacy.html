Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Information we collect

When you search, book or contact us, we collect the details needed to arrange your trip: your name, email address, phone number, the names and dates of birth of the people traveling, passport details where an airline or cruise line requires them, and payment details. We also collect basic technical information when you use the site, such as your browser type and the pages you visit.

How we use it

We use your information to:

  • book and manage your flights, hotels, car rentals and cruises;
  • send booking confirmations, reminders and changes to your trip;
  • answer your questions and help when plans change;
  • prevent fraud and keep payments secure;
  • send offers, only if you have asked us to. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Who we share it with

To complete a booking we pass the necessary details to the airline, hotel, car rental company or cruise line you choose, and to our payment provider. We never sell your personal information. We may share information when the law requires it.

Cookies

We use cookies to keep you signed in, remember your searches and understand how the site is used. You can turn cookies off in your browser settings; some features, such as saved searches, may then stop working.

How long we keep it

We keep booking records for as long as tax and travel regulations require, usually up to seven years. Other information is deleted when it is no longer needed.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted, and you can object to marketing at any time. Write to us at privacy@example.com and we will reply within 30 days.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email privacy@example.com or call +1 917 555 0100. Our full address is on the contact page.
