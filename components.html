Components

The building blocks of this template, each with the markup to copy. Styles are in css/style.css (and css/pages.css for the newer pieces); behavior is in js/script.js.

Colors

The whole template uses one small palette. There are no CSS variables in v1: to change a color, replace its value everywhere in css/style.css.

  • Teal dark#00a99d
  • Teal deep#00857c
  • Mint#e3f6f5
  • Mint light#e8f8f7
  • Red#e8202a
  • Yellow#f7c016
  • Light yellow#fff799
  • Grey 1#464646
  • Grey 2#636363
  • Grey 3#959595
  • Grey 4#ebebeb
  • Grey 5#fafafa

Form fields

Put a native <select> in .select1_inner and a date input in .input1_inner. js/script.js turns them into the template’s dropdown and date range picker automatically; the form still submits the native values. Two date fields in one form pick a range.

Accordion

Native <details> elements, so it works without JavaScript. Add data-accordion to the wrapper to keep one answer open at a time (js/pages.js).

Do I need a visa?

Citizens of the US, the UK, Canada and the EU can stay up to one year without a visa.

How many days do I need?

Five days covers the capital and the mountains. Ten nights adds wine country and the coast.

Can I pay in euros?

Yes, by card in US dollars, euros or pounds.

    <summary>Do I need a visa?</summary>

  </details>
