About this hotel

This five-star hotel stands on a quiet street in Prague, a short tram ride from Old Town Square and Charles Bridge. Rooms are bright and spacious, with large windows, soundproofing, air conditioning and very comfortable beds, and many look out over the rooftops toward Prague Castle. A full buffet breakfast with hot dishes, fresh pastries and local specialties is served every morning in the main restaurant.

The main dining room serves Czech and international dishes from an open kitchen, and the lobby bar offers light meals, local beers and cocktails until late. The wellness area has an indoor pool, a sauna, a steam room and a fitness center open 24 hours a day. The concierge can arrange anything from dinner reservations to day trips to Kutna Hora or Cesky Krumlov, and the nearest tram stop is two minutes away on foot.
