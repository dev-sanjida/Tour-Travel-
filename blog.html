Island Hopping in the Cyclades

April 16, 2026
Posted by George Smith / 26 Comments
L

ast May we spent seven days moving between four Greek islands with nothing but a carry-on each. Ferries in the Cyclades run often in early summer, and booking them a week ahead was enough. We started on Naxos, where the beaches are long and the crowds are thin.

Paros came next, a short hop away, then quiet Folegandros, and finally Santorini for two nights of sunsets before the flight home.

Book the first and last nights in advance and leave the middle of the trip loose. The islands reward travelers who linger.
George Smith Travel Agency

Moving between the islands is the easy part. Most ferries take between one and three hours, and the fast boats are worth the extra fare on longer routes. What needs planning is where you sleep. Small family hotels fill up early in July and August, so in peak season reserve rooms at least two months ahead. In May, June and September you have more choice, lower prices and water that is already warm enough for swimming. We kept one simple rule: never more than two short stays in a row, followed by at least three nights somewhere to unpack and slow down.

If you want help putting a route like this together, our team can book the flights, ferries and hotels as one trip, so a delayed boat never costs you a room. Ask for our Cyclades itinerary and we will adjust it to your dates, budget and pace. Most travelers pay less than they would booking each piece on their own, and everything sits under one confirmation number.

How to Pack for a Cruise

April 16, 2026
Posted by George Smith / 26 Comments

Packing for a cruise is not like packing for a hotel stay. Cabins are compact, closet space is limited, and you will be dressing for beach days, shore excursions and at least one formal dinner. The good news is that you only unpack once. Start with a list built around your route: warm-weather sailings need swimwear, light layers and sun protection, while northern routes call for a waterproof jacket and comfortable walking shoes for cold, rainy ports.

Bring a small day bag for excursions, a refillable water bottle and copies of your passport and travel insurance. Keep medication, documents and one change of clothes in your carry-on, because checked bags can take a few hours to reach your cabin on the first day. Our cruise specialists send every guest a packing list for their route a week before departure.
