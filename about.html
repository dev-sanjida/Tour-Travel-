WHAT WE OFFER ?

Everything you need for a trip, booked in one place and backed by people who travel
themselves and know the places we sell.

  • Flights on more than 400 airlines, with seats and bag fees shown up front;
  • Hotels and apartments we have checked, from city stays to resorts;
  • Car rentals with clear insurance options and no surprise fees at the desk.
We started this agency because we were tired of booking trips in five different places and still not knowing who to call when something went wrong. Today our travel specialists plan everything in one booking: the flight, the hotel, the car waiting at the airport and the cruise at the end of the trip. Every itinerary is checked by a person before it is confirmed, and every traveler gets a direct line to the team that planned it, at any hour.

ABOUT OUR COMPANY
