About this rental

Pick-up is at the rental desk in the arrivals hall, open from 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM. Please bring a driving license held for at least two years, your passport and a credit card in the main driver's name. A second driver can be added at the desk for a small daily fee, and child seats can be reserved in advance.

If your flight is delayed, the desk holds the car for up to two hours as long as your flight number is in the booking. Mileage is unlimited within the country, and trips across the border must be approved in advance. Our team is available 24/7 if you need to change dates, extend the rental or add extra cover.

What the price covers

Included

  • All mandatory fees and airport surcharge
  • VAT and local taxes
  • Unlimited mileage within the country
  • Free cancellation up to 48 hours before pick-up

Not included

  • Damage and collision protection, $372 per rental
  • Personal accident protection, $50 per rental
  • Fuel: return the car full, or pay for refuelling
  • Second driver and child seats, paid at the desk
