Marketing Manager

Jynda Martin

Jynda joined the agency eleven years ago as a travel writer and now runs our marketing, from the weekly deals newsletter to the destination guides on this site. She still writes most of those guides herself, after visiting every place she recommends.

Her favorite trips are slow ones: a week on the Amalfi Coast with no plans, or a train across the Swiss Alps with a good book. Ask her about small hotels in southern Europe and she will have three names for you before you finish the question.

Specialties

  • Boutique hotels
  • Rail journeys
  • Food and wine
